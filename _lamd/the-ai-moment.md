\section{The AI moment}

\include{_atomic-human/includes/gods-and-robots-scribeysense.md}

\newslide{The AI moment (executive framing)}
\slides{
* The “AI moment” was a branding + investment shift: modern machine learning became “AI”, and expectations jumped ahead of delivery reality.
* Don’t confuse **intelligent systems** (scaled decision-making) with **intelligent entities** (robot-people).
* For leaders, the question is practical: what decisions are being automated, with what data, and who bears the downside when it’s wrong?
}

\newslide{Four executive questions (default frame)}
\slides{
* What decision are we changing?
* What information does it require (and what’s missing)?
* How can it fail (and how will we notice early)?
* Who is accountable (and what is the escalation path)?
}
