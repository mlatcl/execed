# Tenet: Move fast with guardrails

## Tenet

**Title**: Move fast with guardrails

**Description**: Good governance enables speed. We reduce friction by making decision rights explicit and embedding lightweight guardrails (data access, privacy, security, model risk) into the workflow. The goal is not “perfect control”, but fast learning without avoidable harm.

**Quote**: *"Speed comes from clarity, not shortcuts."*

**Examples**:
- Establish a pre-approved “safe sandbox” for experimentation with clear data classifications and access controls.
- Use standard checklists for high-risk changes (privacy impact, security review, model monitoring) that teams can self-serve.
- Define escalation paths: what must be approved, by whom, and within what SLA.

**Counter-examples**:
- Governance as a late-stage gate that arrives after investment and excitement, forcing rework.
- Ambiguous approvals that drive shadow IT and inconsistent risk decisions.
- “No guardrails” experimentation that creates incidents and destroys trust.

**Conflicts**:
- Potential conflict with **strategy-before-tools** when speed is mistaken for progress.
- Resolution: Guardrails must include a minimal strategy/value check (decision, owner, value hypothesis) in addition to risk controls.
