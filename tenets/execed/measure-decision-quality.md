# Tenet: Measure decision quality, not just model metrics

## Tenet

**Title**: Measure decision quality, not just model metrics

**Description**: The point of analytics and AI is better decisions and execution. We prefer measures that track adoption, time-to-decision, error rates, exception handling, and business outcomes—not only technical performance. This keeps leaders focused on whether the organisation is *behaving differently* and getting better results.

**Quote**: *"Accuracy is a means; outcomes are the test."*

**Examples**:
- Track: who uses the tool, how often, what decisions it influenced, and what changed as a result.
- Use leading indicators (cycle time, rework, exception rates) alongside lagging outcomes (revenue, cost, risk events).
- Audit “decision override” patterns to detect misalignment, bias, or incentives problems.

**Counter-examples**:
- Celebrating high AUC/accuracy while frontline teams ignore outputs or work around the system.
- Measuring only pilot ROI without checking sustainability (support load, incidents, drift, training needs).
- Ignoring organisational side-effects (gaming metrics, degraded trust, perverse incentives).

**Conflicts**:
- Can conflict with **strategy-before-tools** if teams pick easy-to-measure metrics instead of strategic outcomes.
- Resolution: Start with the strategic decision and define a balanced scorecard (outcome + adoption + risk).
