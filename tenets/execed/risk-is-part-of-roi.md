# Tenet: Risk is part of ROI

## Tenet

**Title**: Risk is part of ROI

**Description**: Executive evaluation includes downside, not just upside. We treat privacy, security, regulatory exposure, reputational harm, and model risk as *first-class* components of business cases and prioritisation. This reframes “ethics” from a moral add-on into disciplined enterprise risk management.

**Quote**: *"If you don’t price the downside, you’re not doing ROI."*

**Examples**:
- Use a “risk-adjusted value hypothesis”: expected benefit minus expected loss (likelihood × impact) plus mitigation cost.
- Define acceptable error and harm thresholds upfront (e.g., for credit, hiring, fraud), and monitor them continuously.
- Include third-party and supply-chain risk when assessing vendors (data sharing, auditability, exit costs).

**Counter-examples**:
- Treating privacy/security as a compliance checkbox to be handled after deployment.
- Ignoring reputational risk because it’s “hard to quantify”.
- Optimising for short-term cost savings while accumulating long-term regulatory and trust debt.

**Conflicts**:
- Potential conflict with **move-fast-with-guardrails** if risk review becomes slow or vague.
- Resolution: Standardise risk tiers and self-serve mitigations so speed improves *without* skipping risk.
