# REQ-0001: Execed course reflects *The Atomic Human* and the post-2024 AI/agent landscape

## Description

The executive education course materials and narrative must reflect the completed framing of *The Atomic Human* and incorporate the material changes in the environment since mid‑2024 (widespread LLM use, coding agents, and new human–agent interaction failure modes).

This is an outcome requirement: it does not prescribe which lectures change or how they change, only that the course’s framing and content are recognisably updated for today’s decision-making reality.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The course landing (`index.html`) and repository overview (`README.md`) explicitly position the course as inspired by *The Atomic Human* and relevant to the current AI/agent landscape.
- [ ] The first lecture (“The New World”, or its renamed replacement) introduces the updated framing (capability vs entity, uncertainty, trust/accountability, human–agent interaction) in executive language.
- [ ] At least one lecture explicitly addresses LLMs/agents: what they enable, where they fail, and what governance/measurement leaders need.
- [ ] The revised structure and narrative is consistent with the exec tenets in `tenets/execed/`.

## Notes (Optional)

This requirement is expected to be implemented via a CIP that performs a course re-architecture and lecture refresh.
